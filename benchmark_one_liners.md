## Key Points to Emphasize

1. **Be honest about costs** - opticode costs more in tokens
2. **Emphasize time savings** - 6.7 hours/month is the real value
3. **Quality over cost** - Better first-try success
4. **Developer experience** - Less frustration, fewer round-trips

## What NOT to Say

❌ "Saves $5/month in tokens" - This is false
❌ "Reduces API costs" - The opposite is true
❌ "Free optimization" - It costs a bit more

## What TO Say

✅ "Saves 6.7 hours/month"
✅ "Improves first-try success to 95%"
✅ "Better developer experience"
✅ "Costs $4 more but saves $667 in time"
